PHASE 01

Landing Spot Strategy

Where you land decides whether you survive the first two minutes. Don't rush into hot drops — pick a strategy that matches your goal.

🌲
Low Risk

Edge-of-Map POI

Smaller locations on the map's edge draw fewer players. Loot in peace and get comfortable with your controls — the tradeoff is a longer rotation into the zone later.

🏚️
Medium Risk

Secondary POI

Not the hottest drop, but still solid loot with moderate foot traffic. Good for players who want to practice fights without instantly getting third-partied.

🔥
High Risk

Named Landmark / Hot Drop

Best loot, but everyone else knows it too. You'll be in a gunfight the second you land. Not recommended for beginners unless you specifically want aim practice.

PHASE 02

Loot Priority

Item color = rarity. The further right on the scale, the stronger the stats. Check the color before you waste inventory space on it.

COMMON
Grey/White
Fine in a pinch — grab it early if nothing better is around
UNCOMMON
Green

RARE
Blue
Your mid-game main — worth swapping out any green
EPIC
Purple
Grab it on sight — clear jump in fire rate/damage over blue
LEGENDARY
Gold
Top tier for the map — always pick it up

▸ Recommended Looting Order for Beginners

  1. Shield items (small/large shield potions) — top priority, you're paper without shield
  2. One ranged + one close-range weapon — AR/sniper paired with a shotgun covers every distance
  3. Healing items (bandages/med kits) — carry enough for at least two full heals before moving on
  4. Building materials — grab them naturally as you loot, don't detour just to farm mats
  5. Throwables (grenades/traps) — nice to have if you have space, not a priority

01

Box Up First

Getting shot at? Instinct number one: four walls plus a roof/ramp to fully enclose yourself before deciding what to do next. Never trade shots standing in the open.

02

Ramp Rush

Chain ramps quickly to gain height fast, grabbing the high-ground advantage. A core move for both pushing and escaping.

03

Edit, Don't Destroy

Need a window or a doorway? Use the edit function to cut a hole rather than breaking the whole wall — you keep your structure intact and stay protected.

04

Don't Over-Farm Mats

Around 200 of each material is enough for most fights. You don't need to cap out at 999 — that extra time is better spent looting or repositioning.

PHASE 04

Mid-to-Late Game Tactics

Once you're past the early game, it's less about reflexes and more about game sense — reading the storm, holding position, and watching your back.

STORM READING

Rotate Early

Don't wait until the storm is closing in to move. Plan your route the moment the next circle is announced, and prioritize edge-of-zone or high-ground spots while keeping mats in reserve.

THIRD-PARTIES

Watch for the Third Party

Gunfire gives away both players' positions. If a fight runs past 10 seconds, start checking your flanks and rear — build cover while you fight if a third squad might be closing in.

POSITIONING

Take High Ground / Cover

Endgame fights are won on terrain. Take the long way around to grab a high perch rather than trading shots in the open.

PACING

Don't Fight Every Fight

Not every encounter is worth taking. If the enemy clearly out-guns or out-numbers you, rotating away and surviving to the next circle is a win too.
